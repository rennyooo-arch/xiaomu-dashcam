# Privacy Policy · XiaoMu Dashcam

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Developer: Ren XiaoMu

---

## The short version

XiaoMu Dashcam does not collect, upload, or share any of your data. There is no server, no account, no ads, and no analytics. Your recordings live on your phone, and only on your phone.

## What data we collect

**None.**

This app has no backend server and no user account system, and it embeds no third-party data-collection, advertising, or analytics SDKs. The app never sends your personal information, recordings, location, or usage behavior anywhere.

## How permissions are used

Every system permission the app requests is used only on-device, only for the feature it belongs to:

| Permission | Purpose | Where the data goes |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Front and rear video recording (the core dashcam function) | Video is stored only on your device |
| Microphone | Audio recorded together with the video | Audio is stored only on your device, as part of the video |
| Location (GPS) | The GPS coordinate / speed watermark on the video, and a matching GPX track file for each clip | Coordinates and tracks are stored only on your device; never transmitted |
| Storage (file read/write) | Saving, reading, and deleting video clips and track files; exporting evidence packs | Files stay in the local folder you choose |
| Motion sensors (accelerometer and similar system capabilities) | Collision detection that triggers automatic locking of the current clip | Evaluated in real time on-device only; not recorded or transmitted |

If you decline any permission, only the corresponding feature becomes unavailable. The app will not nag you for it, and no data is rerouted anywhere else.

## Data storage and deletion

- All video clips and GPX track files are stored locally on your phone (in the app's folder or a folder you choose).
- Loop recording automatically overwrites the oldest unlocked clips according to your storage setting; locked clips are never overwritten.
- You can delete any clip at any time inside the app, delete the files directly in a file manager, or uninstall the app and erase its data.
- Deletion is entirely under your control. We keep no copies, and we cannot recover or delete your files for you.

## Data sharing

The app never shares anything on its own. Files leave your device only when you deliberately use the evidence-pack export or a system share action, and only through the channel you pick (email, messaging app, etc.). After that, the receiving service's own rules apply; the app is not involved.

## Third-party services

The app integrates no third-party SDKs of any kind — no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no social SDKs. The app does not depend on any network service to function.

## Children's privacy

This app is intended for adult drivers. It is not designed for children, and it does not (and technically cannot) collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes features in a way that requires updating this policy, we will revise this page and update the effective date at the top. Any significant change affecting data collection will be prominently announced inside the app.
